# Helpdesk System — Feature Wishlist
**Source:** Sam Rivera, IT Manager
**Context:** IT helpdesk currently runs on email. Volume has tripled in 18 months.

## The Wishlist (unranked)

1. Ticket creation via web form
2. Ticket creation via email (auto-convert to ticket)
3. Ticket assignment to specific agents
4. Priority levels (P1–P4)
5. SLA tracking and alerts
6. Knowledge base / FAQ integration
7. Self-service password reset (no ticket needed)
8. Reporting dashboard (tickets by category, agent, resolution time)
9. Customer satisfaction survey after resolution
10. Slack integration (notifications to #it-helpdesk channel)
11. Mobile app for agents
12. Asset management integration (link ticket to device)

## Manager's Context

"Email is chaos. I can't tell who's working on what. I have no idea what our resolution time is. I hired two new agents last month and I still can't see what they're doing. We had a P1 outage go unnoticed for 45 minutes because it was buried in the inbox."
