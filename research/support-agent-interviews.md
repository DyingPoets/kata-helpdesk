# Support Agent Interviews — Key Quotes
**Conducted by:** [Your name]
**Date:** [Session date]

---

**Agent 1 — Jamie (2 years on team):**
"Every morning I spend 20 minutes going through email to figure out what's new, what's been answered, and what's been sitting there for three days. Half the time something slips through because it looks like it was already replied to but it wasn't resolved."

---

**Agent 2 — Taylor (6 months on team):**
"When I pick up a ticket that someone else started, I have no idea what's happened. Did they call the user? Is it waiting on the user? Is it waiting on me? I have to read the whole email thread from the beginning every time."

---

**Agent 3 — Riley (4 years on team):**
"Password resets. That's 40% of my day. Easy to fix, but I still have to do them manually because there's no self-service. I could be working on real problems."

---

**Agent 4 — Alex (1 year on team):**
"I missed an SLA last week. Nobody told me what our SLAs even are. I found out when the manager mentioned it in the team meeting. How am I supposed to hit targets I don't know about?"

---

**Agent 5 — Morgan (3 years on team):**
"The worst part is when a user emails asking for an update. I have nothing to tell them. I don't know where it stands in the queue. I have to go dig through emails to find out, which takes longer than just fixing the problem sometimes."
